# 🎓 Student Assistant – Your All-in-One Smart Student Platform

**One Platform. Every Student Need.**

Student Assistant is a modern, AI-powered educational platform designed to make student life easier, smarter, and more productive. It brings essential academic tools, intelligent AI assistance, document management, productivity features, and creative utilities together in one unified platform.

Our mission is to simplify student life through technology and make powerful digital tools accessible to students everywhere.

---

## 🌟 Project Overview

Students often rely on multiple applications for their academic and personal needs. Student Assistant aims to solve this problem by providing an all-in-one digital environment where students can manage their academic tasks, access intelligent assistance, organize documents, and improve productivity.

Whether you are a school, college, or university student, Student Assistant aims to be your digital companion throughout your educational journey.
